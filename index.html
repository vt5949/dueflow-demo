Frequently asked questions

Who is this designed for?

Industrial job-work suppliers, machining companies and subcontracted manufacturers evaluating their billing workflows.

Can I use real business documents?

No. This preview is for invented records only. Secure accounts, production data protection and independent financial validation are not available.

Which files are supported?

Structured fictional CSV files with the specified headers. Excel workbooks, PDFs and scanned invoices are not automatically extracted.

Does it guarantee revenue recovery?

No. Findings are arithmetic review candidates. Contract adjustments and source evidence must be verified separately.

Can I share feedback?

Email hello.arovendi@gmail.com with general comments only. Do not send financial records, private information or payments.
