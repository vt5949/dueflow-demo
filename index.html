Arovendi · job-work billing audit

Find billable work that may have been missed

Compare an approved job order, buyer-accepted quantities and pre-tax invoiced amounts. For small industrial job-work suppliers. Use invented data only. This is a deterministic reconciliation check, not an accounting or tax opinion.

One row per job in each CSV; aggregate split invoices first. Whole units only. Values exclude GST, freight, advances, retention and other adjustments. Explicitly enter 0 if confirmed unbilled; a missing row means unknown. This demo does not connect to Tally or read PDFs.
