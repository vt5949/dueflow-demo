Questions

Is my data safe?

The health check runs entirely in your browser; nothing is uploaded. For pilots we agree a written data-handling note, collect only what’s needed and delete it on request.

My team already sends reminders from Tally. Why Dueflow?

Sending is the easy part. Dueflow decides who to chase today, in what order and in what tone, and keeps a record of every promise, so reminders turn into payments.

Will this upset my customers?

You set the tone and can exclude key accounts. Messages are polite and factual, and the amounts come from your own data.

What is the interest estimate?

The MSMED Act provides for interest on payments delayed beyond the agreed period (at most 45 days) to Udyam-registered micro and small suppliers. The tool shows an indicative figure at three times the RBI bank rate with monthly rests. Check with your CA or a lawyer before claiming it.

Who is behind this?

A founder-led team in India. Book a call and you’ll talk to the person who does the work.
